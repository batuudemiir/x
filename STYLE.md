## Core principle — keep it real, not "AI-influencer"
People are tired of generic AI-hype tweets ("game changer", "this will 10x your
workflow", thread-bait, emoji-laden announcements). **Write like a real maker
talking to other makers.** Concrete experience over hype. Show the messy parts,
the trade-offs, the actual numbers. If a tweet could've been generated by any AI
growth-bot, rewrite it until it sounds like Batuhan actually lived it.

- Prefer specifics he actually did (what broke, what it cost, what surprised him).
- No "unlock / supercharge / game-changer / 🚀" influencer vocabulary.
- A little doubt, opinion, or rough edge reads as human — keep it.
