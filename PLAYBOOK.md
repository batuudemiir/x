# Growth Playbook — AI-news commentary (@wakebatuhan)

Strategy: grow by posting **early, real takes on current AI news**, riding the
trend's wave and bigger accounts' audiences. Fits Batuhan's existing habit
(quote-tweeting Claude/Fable 5 news) and the 2026 algorithm (recency + tone +
conversation). Pair with STYLE.md (keep it real, not AI-hype).

## Why this works at <1,000 followers
- A standalone tweet reaches ~7 followers. A **quote-tweet or reply on the
  source account** (e.g. @claudeai, @OpenAI, news accounts) rides *their*
  audience → cheapest out-of-network exposure (the main lever at this size).
- News has a 30–60 min wave; being early + present for replies = riding it.
- Real, specific maker takes clear Grok's quality/tone screens; hype doesn't.

## Daily loop (runnable from this chat)
1. **Spot** — from your feed, or ask me "bugün ne var?" and I pull the top AI stories.
2. **Pick 1–2** stories that fit the niche (AI tooling, Claude ecosystem,
   builder-relevant, model releases, anything you've actually used).
3. **Angle** — choose a real angle (templates below), never "this changes everything 🚀".
4. **Format** — default to **quote-tweeting / replying to the source big account**,
   not a standalone. Standalones only for things you genuinely lived.
5. **Post early**, then **reply to every reply in the first hour** (fires the +75
   author-engagement loop).
6. **Plus 1–2 quality replies** to other big takes on the same news (more surface).

## Angle templates (real > hype)
- **Builder's so-what:** "tek başına app yapan biri için bunun anlamı şu: …"
- **Tried it, honest result:** "denedim, gerçek sonuç: …" (numbers, rough edges)
- **Overhyped / underhyped:** "herkes X diyor ama asıl olay Y"
- **The part nobody mentions:** "kimse şunu konuşmuyor: …"
- **Connect two stories:** "X + Y aynı hafta çıktı; birleşince …"
- **Vendor-risk / maker reality:** practical consequence for someone shipping solo.
- **Contrarian-but-constructive:** disagree with the hype, kindly, with a reason.

## Rules
- Lowercase, casual TR, one concrete idea, end conversation-posts with an
  answerable question.
- No hype vocabulary (game-changer, unlock, 🚀🧵), no engagement bait, ≤1 hashtag.
- Don't tweet about a tool you haven't touched — borrow others' takes via replies instead.
- Consistency > volume: 1–2 sharp news takes/day + 3–5 quality replies beats bursting.
